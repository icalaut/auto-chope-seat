Safety, privacy & how it works

The app prepares the browser 5 minutes early. If the form is unavailable, preparation retries every 30 seconds until one minute before retrieval. It never retries a retrieval click. Local dispatch checks refuse early attempts and attempts more than 50 ms late; operating-system pauses and network delays mean exact execution and server receipt times cannot be guaranteed.

If Scoot assigns a queue, reports unsuccessful web check-in, check-in not open yet or already closed, PNR not found, or a departure mismatch, automation stops. Attend the airport staff-travel counter in person to queue and wait as instructed. The app never retries, refreshes, or performs further check-in actions after retrieval.

PNR is held in memory only and sent to Scoot when the browser opens before retrieval. If a passport/nationality form appears, a screenshot, page snapshot and dropdown inventory are saved locally for future implementation. These may contain personal booking data; keep them private. Retrieve Booking does not reserve or confirm a seat; review the result yourself. Quitting the app cancels pending work.

The app stops if it cannot identify or verify the form. It does not bypass maintenance, CAPTCHA, queues, or check-in opening times.
