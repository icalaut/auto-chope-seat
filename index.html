Schedule one Retrieve Booking click. Five minutes before the retrieval time, the app opens Scoot, fills your entries and stands by, then clicks once on time.

Live integration is not yet verified: Scoot was under maintenance during development. The app stops if it cannot identify or verify the form. It does not bypass maintenance, CAPTCHA, queues, or check-in opening times.

Timezone is automatically selected from the departure station. For an unrecognized station, select its airport timezone manually. IANA zones account for daylight saving time.

Manual cloud-browser controls

Available only after retrieval, for the remaining session time. Passport and nationality entries are manual. Actions go to the cloud browser, not a new local tab. Nothing here guarantees boarding; follow Scoot's counter instructions.

Use only one device for this booking. Keep this app running and the laptop awake, plugged in and open (the app prevents idle sleep, but closing the lid still sleeps). You may close this page; reopen the app to check status. If the form is not ready, preparation retries every 30 seconds until one minute before retrieval. Network and browser delays mean exact server receipt time cannot be guaranteed. Missed clicks over two seconds late are stopped.

If Scoot assigns a queue, reports unsuccessful web check-in, check-in not open yet or already closed, PNR not found, or a departure mismatch, automation stops. Attend the airport staff-travel counter in person to queue and wait as instructed. The app never retries, refreshes, or performs further check-in actions after retrieval.

PNR is held in memory only and sent to Scoot when the browser opens before retrieval. No booking data is saved to files or browser storage. Retrieve Booking does not reserve or confirm a seat; review the result yourself. Quitting the app cancels pending work.
